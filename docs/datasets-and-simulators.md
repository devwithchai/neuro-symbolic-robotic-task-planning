# Datasets and Simulators

Candidate environments to evaluate during research planning:

- Gazebo / ROS 2 Humble
- TurtleBot3 simulation
- AI2-THOR
- Habitat
- iGibson
- BEHAVIOR
- ManiSkill
- PyBullet
- MuJoCo
- MoveIt 2 ecosystem

The project will prioritize environments that are computationally feasible on the development machine. Large GPU-dependent simulation stacks should not become hard dependencies.

A final benchmark/environment selection will be based on:

- task-planning relevance
- reproducibility
- licensing/access
- compute requirements
- available baselines
- compatibility with the research question
